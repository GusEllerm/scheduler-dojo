### What the rig found (three real things, all in the app's favour)

1. **Two buttons are labelled "Campus (live)"** — the top-level mode picker (`main.ts` titles it from
   `MODE_NOTES`) and the campus's own live/hand variant chip. From *inside* campus mode the picker
   button is a no-op (`setMode` returns when the mode is unchanged), so a driver that clicks the
   first match silently does nothing. The rig targets `.campus-variant-bar button`. Wording worth a
   human look: the two controls say the same thing but do different jobs.
2. **A city week's boundary is its horizon at city 1.** The live run freezes there for the offer
   choice and does not end until a decision is taken (`Week end — offers` is the hatch). A driver
   that does not take an offer waits forever — correctly: the game is asking something.
3. **A skipped script keeps ownership of its endings** (`onFinish` only auto-opens the review when no
   runner is on stage), so the review comes from the readout's "Review ▸". The rig accepts either
   path, but only after `snap.done` says this run actually ended — otherwise a stale review from the
   previous run would pass the gate.

No product fix was needed for any of the three; nothing in this pass changed `src/` or `web/src/`.

## Review-8 close-out (the Art 8 adversarial pass)

Seven findings, all real, all fixed — each proven by the committed rigs (62/62 a11y,
12/12 playthrough, art7b evidence ok, 327 pytest, baselines byte-identical):

1. Space/S/1-2-3 fired behind the Upgrade Shop — `.shop-overlay` joins the dialog veto, and
   `anyDialogOpen` is now VISIBILITY-aware (the shop hides rather than removes its overlay — a
   naive selector made every key dead forever; the audit caught what the review could not).
2. Space unpaused a week-frozen campus — the freeze now vetoes Pause/Play regardless of `paused`;
   the only way out of a frozen week is an offer choice.
3. A dismissed share card could hijack the mode — the replay retires itself if the panel is gone
   (the button no longer dismisses on click; dismissal lands with the replay).
4. Repeat `#card=` links in an open tab were swallowed — dismiss clears the fragment (defensively:
   a throw there once ate the whole replay chain; the rig proved the regression AND the fix), and
   only a card→nothing transition closes the view.
5. `dialogStack` retained every closed dialog (detached panels + closures pinned until reload) —
   entries now drop themselves on every close path.
6. The welcome card gained a "Later" door + backdrop dismiss — an exit that starts nothing.
7. The card VIEW's continuations got the `isConnected` guards their mint sibling already had.
