# 2026-10-02 Phase 2 Art 7 — part a: the endless campus and the run review

Art 7 §2.5/§2.6 in two parts. **Part a** (this note) is *endless on the board and the review
screen*: the seeded growing city running live on the campus, the overflow moment that ends it, and
the calm post-run panel — with the leaderboard-of-one best score. Part b (below) is the city
board and share cards in the new art. Mapping authority stays in [[Concepts/Campus]] (its "The endless campus and the
review screen (Art 7a)" section); the bridge/stepping facts in [[Pyodide Bridge]] and
[[scheduler_dojo-sim-scheduler]].

The previous builder wired the client (`web/src/review.ts`, the endless driver in
`campus-play.ts`/`main.ts`, the `endlessLevel`/`overflow` bridge fields, this rig) but died before
evidence/docs; part a reconciles that work, exercises it end to end, and fixes what the exercise
turned up.

## What shipped

- **`web/src/review.ts`** — the ONE review panel. Opens itself at the end of a campus run (live,
  hand, endless) and is offered as a "Review ▸" button (`mountReviewButton`) on every other
  finished run's readout, so no modal fires the instant a watch page loads. It decides nothing and
  calls nothing: the strip is the phase-one `mountTimeline` mounted **paused** (dark cells are the
  idle gaps), the share rows are the rail's `fairnessShares` re-rendered from the finished job list,
  and the mistake lines are *derived* — timeouts, never-parked, starved shares, and the overflow
  line. `mistakesOf` aggregates per (user, kind) so an endless overflow that strands hundreds of
  jobs is one calm line, not a thousand.
- **`web/src/campus-play.ts`** — `endlessMode` (recognized by `id === "endless"`): a **literal**
  86,400 s / 7-day calendar (cities keep the compressed one-week mapping), week boundaries that
  advance the clock but never freeze (the offer table is keyed by integer city, there is no endless
  key), the `afterDone` → `overflowMoment` hold (one ring-pulse flash of the overflowing neighbour's
  road vehicles, `.campus-overflow-flash`, ~0.9 s; the visual harness never flashes, reduced motion
  finishes instantly), and the `seekRender` done-routing through `afterDone`. The endless campus
  also passes `simRate` (below).
- **`web/src/main.ts`** — the Endless chip launches the live endless run (`bridge.endlessLevel` of
  `ENDLESS_GROWTH` at the save's seed, booth staffed with the engine's `shortest_first`), a "New
  seed ▸" reroll (a UI-side LCG step from the shown integer — `Math.random` never touches a
  sim-bound stream, [[Determinism]]), the best score (`prefs.endlessBest`, days first / served as
  tie-break) surfaced on the chip, and `openReviewPanel` wired into `onFinish` for both endless and
  city runs (a script owns its own ending, so it is skipped while one is on stage).
- **`web/src/style.css`** — the review overlay/panel/mistake-row/overflow-flash rules (fixed
  overlay so the panel works in every mode's chrome; the share rows reuse the rail's classes).

## Two client bugs the exercise caught (not in the previous builder's claims)

1. **`fairnessShares` NaN in the review** — every share read "0% · NaNhNaNm parked" and *every*
   neighbour was flagged `starved`. Cause: **Pyodide's `toJs` turns Python `None` into
   `undefined`**, but `fairnessShares` only treats `null` as never-started, so an unfinished endless
   job poisoned every share sum with NaN. Phase one already guards this exact quirk (`main.ts:982`,
   `timeline.ts:89`); the review's `shareJobs` now null-normalizes `start`/`end`. (The city review
   never showed it because its run had no never-started job.)
2. **`step_result` reported the wrong policy** — the review meta said `fifo` for a run the campus
   started with `shortest_first`, because `step_result` read `lvl.default_policy` (the endless level
   has none). `bridge.start` now records the policy it actually started with (`_SESSION_POLICIES`)
   and `step_result` reports it, mirroring what `run()` already did. Verified: the review now reads
   `seed 20261002 · shortest_first`.

## The "step_until stall" — what it actually was

An earlier investigation of the endless campus freezing at `t=1080` under `seekRender` after a
pause chased an app-side race. There was none. It was two things: (1) a **driver artifact** —
playwright silently does *not* invoke a block/expression-bodied `async (t) => …` **string** passed
to `page.evaluate` (it returns `undefined`), so the first pass's "frozen" seeks never ran at all;
every seek here is a real function. (2) the **real pin**, an engine one: a driver whose step target
sat below `t0 + horizon` on a t0>0 level pinned the clock forever. The engine half was fixed in
`4cab6b3` (stepped runs END at the horizon, done fires on the stop frame, snapshots publish
`horizon_end`). This slice added the two ends that fix needs to actually hold on the campus:

- **Client** (`simTarget`): the step-target cap is now `max(duration + 2·step, horizon_end)`, so it
  can never sit below the horizon.
- **Engine** (`_advance`, `4cab6b3` follow-up): the *empty-heap* done path now lands the observed
  clock on `t0 + horizon` before reporting done (the `next-event-past-until` path already did). A
  run whose **last event predates the horizon** — a normal city run — otherwise reported done with
  `now` stuck short of the boundary, and the campus week-end freeze watches `now`, so the run ended
  *without ever offering its week end*. This was the real blocker for the level3 city review case;
  city 3 now freezes at the horizon, takes an offer, then reviews. Test:
  `test_empty_heap_done_lands_the_clock_on_the_horizon` (+1 → **324 passed**).

## Endless pacing (`simRate`) — an [agent decision]

The watch plan's 1× cadence (`step × 60` ≈ 64,800 sim-s/s) is tuned for a compressed city week but
crushes a LITERAL 30-day endless stream into ~40 s of watching — the ramp never reads as a ramp and
a "week 2" screenshot/fps sample can't catch a live run. The endless campus passes a `simRate`
override = **3,600 sim-s per wall-second** (one literal day ≈ 24 s, a calm watchable run). The
stream and its hashes are untouched; city pacing is untouched (the override is endless-only). The
week-thickening then arrives on a schedule the rig can wait on.

# Part b: the city board, and share cards in the new art

Art 7's remaining two deliverables (§2.7 board, §2.8/§5.8/§7 share). Mapping authority is the
"[[Concepts/Campus]] → The city board and the share card (Art 7b)" section; this note is what
happened, what was decided, and what the build proved.

## What shipped

- **`web/src/board.ts`** (`openCityBoard`) — a "City board" chip in the campus toolbar opens one
  `role="dialog"` panel of ten tiles: cities 1–9 plus Endless. Each tile carries a real painted
  campus frame, the city's verdict and best score, and a lock state; tapping an unlocked tile starts
  that city through the *same* chaining rules as "Next city ▸" (`startCampusRun` + the tutorial patch
  + `prefs.city`), so the board cannot fork the campaign. A locked tile answers in the panel's
  `role="status"` line with the way out instead of doing nothing.
- **`web/src/campus-thumb.ts`** (`campusThumbFrame`, `paintCampusThumbnail`) — one painter for the
  board's tiles and the card's thumbnail: `startRun` → 6 × `step_until` → `buildScene` → an offscreen
  `CampusRenderer` → `drawImage` into whatever canvas asked. Cached per page session, offscreen so no
  canvas ever exists for `resizeNow()` to measure.
- **`web/src/share-scene.ts`** — `mintCampusShare` (one `share_encode` call, the campus thumbnail at
  55 % of the run's own traffic, `<origin><pathname>#card=…`), `mountCampusShareButton` (the review
  row's button, phase one's copy box included), `shareCardParam` (read `#card=`, fall back to phase
  one's `?c=`/`#c=`), and `openShareCardView` — decode client-side for the metadata, ask the engine
  (`share_replay`) for the verdict, badge or banner, and only then a "Watch the replay ▸" button.
- **`web/src/review.ts`** — an optional `share` context adds the button to the actions row. Endless
  runs get none (see the deviation below).
- **`web/src/main.ts`** — the board chip and its `onStartCity` chain; `campusRunShare` captures the
  finished run so the review's share button mints *that* run's exact inputs (never the boot-level
  `state.level`); the `#card=` boot route, which loads the card's level for the timeline, opens the
  card, and deliberately **does not** record the replay for progression.
- **`web/scripts/art7b_evidence.mjs`** — four cases, `--case board|share|tamper|legacy`.

## The three review-7a fixes, applied here

- **F1** `web/src/review.ts` — `mistakesOf`/`openReviewPanel` cut the fairness window and the
  "submitted after the horizon" filter at `overflow_time` on an overflow run. `end_time` is the last
  *finish*, so on an overflowed run it leads the instant the ring filled by ~1.6 ks; the old cutoff
  blamed neighbours for vehicles that never reached the road. Checked against the wheel on the
  seeds the evidence uses: level 1 seed 7, seed 12345 and endless seed 424242 all end 1 222–1 636 s
  after overflow, with 30–36 never-parked vehicles inside the window the old code was counting.
- **F3** `web/src/main.ts` — `recordEndlessBest` floors `overflow_time ?? end_time`, so a run that
  died at 22.998 days records 22 days, not 23, and the same number feeds the tile, the chip and the
  review.
- **F4** `web/src/campus-play.ts` — the overflow flash latches (`private flashed`): the ring trip is
  deferred up to 900 ms, so every frame in that window used to re-fire the flash.

## Decisions and deviations, in the open

- **Tiles are framed by the level's own bars**, not the lifetime belt — and with the palette's
  existing judgement roles (`gold` / `ok` / `warn` / dim), not the brief's "silver/bronze", which do
  not exist in `web/tokens/palette.json`. Rationale in [[Concepts/Art Direction]] and
  [[Concepts/Campus]].
- **Endless runs cannot be shared** (brief deviation, deliberate): the stream is materialized — a
  30-day horizon is ~1.9 k jobs — so no URL-sized card describes it, and `share_encode` could not hash
  it either. The endless review and the endless tile say so in words rather than minting a lie.
- **A card view never earns anything**: `flushPendingWatchRecord` is nulled on the `#card=` path, so
  opening someone's link cannot bank a best score or advance the frontier.
- **Hand-played cities share the city, not the placements** — the v1 envelope has no room for
  placements, so the card's tag line says the replay uses the level's own policy (the phase-one
  precedent).
- **Thumbnails use FIFO, not the level's default policy** — level 1's default is `idle`, which parks
  almost nothing and photographs as an empty lot. A tile should show a campus doing something.
- **A first board paint costs about ten watch-length runs**, once, then it is cached for the page
  session (and every frame is painted concurrently — the bridge serializes calls, so the tiles queue
  behind each other on the engine, which is why the dialog says "drawing…" until they land).

## Findings

- **`null` ≠ `None` at the bridge.** `share_encode` had shipped `kata: null` since Stage 9; Pyodide
  turns that into a truthy `JsNull`, so the *non*-kata share path — the common one — had been broken
  the whole time ("expected a module (`jsnull by name:`) or `def`"). `progression_view({state: null})`
  failed the same way. Both now omit the key. Recorded in [[Concepts/Pyodide Bridge]].
- **A card that verifies can still be unrunnable.** `replay_card` runs `validate=False`, so the card's
  embedded `sensors: null` is invisible until the browser *runs* the level and `validate_level`
  rejects it. The card view minted, verified, and then died on the replay button until
  `withoutNulls` was added to the merge. Recorded in [[Modules/scheduler_dojo-share-card]].
- **One jump is not a thumbnail.** `_snapshot.unseen` means a single `step_until` frame renders every
  job submitted before that instant as an unknown-owner, zero-length shell. Six slices, one union —
  the same rule the live loop follows.

## Still open / deferred (part b)

- The board's thumbnails are drawn once per page session and never invalidated by an owned-building
  purchase made *during* that session — the tile for a city you just upgraded shows the campus as of
  the last paint. A reload (or the next session) is correct; an invalidation hook belongs with the
  shop, not here.
- A received card's thumbnail is painted from the level as reconstructed on this machine; if the
  shipped level file changes, the picture and the verdict can disagree (the verdict still wins).
- Endless still has no share card (above); if Art 8 wants one, the honest shape is a *summary* card
  (seed + days survived + score, replay = "grow from this seed") and that is a `share_encode` change.
