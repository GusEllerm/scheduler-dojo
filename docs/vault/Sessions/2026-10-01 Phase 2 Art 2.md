# 2026-10-01 Phase 2 Art 2 — engine additions

§5.1–5.8 of `PROMPT-ART.md`, without touching a single trajectory hash. Core scheduler/bridge edits
by the orchestrator (coherence-critical); calendar/offers/endless/tutorial-loader by a builder.

## What landed

- **Placements in the run record (§5.1)** — `JobResult.placed_nodes`/`run_site` (trailing defaulted
  fields; `trajectory_str` unchanged), `_jobs_json` emits `placed`/`site`, snapshots already carried
  `placed_nodes` and now emit `running[].end` (run+transfer) for animation.
- **Decision trace (§5.2)** — `Scheduler(trace=N)` ring buffer; engine actions (`place`/`preempt`/
  `route`) emit from the scheduler, kata adds `order` (key per ≤12 ranked jobs + who was first) and
  `fallback` (code) via `KataPolicy(tracer=…)`. `trace=0` pays nothing (asserted).
- **Patience rings (§5.3)** — level-gated `pressure {cap, end_on_overflow}`; ring = max over
  unfinished jobs of wait/((cap−1)×claimed est); `overflow_user` + optional run-end; `is_stopped()`
  is the single "over" predicate. Ring levels tick (~duration/70) so rings fill *between* events —
  the first version only computed at event boundaries and froze (test caught it).
- **Reservations/preemptions/transfers in snapshots (§5.4)** — `reserve()` intents mirrored to
  `Scheduler.reservations` by the policy; preemptions/transfers arrive as trace records.
- **Requested vs actual (§5.5)** — `est` always ships; `runtime` ships unless `hide_actual` (the
  sensor visibility rule, enforced at the boundary the same way the builtin enforces it mid-run).
- **Calendar (§5.6)** — `sim/calendar.py` (see [[Campus]] for the decision); `bridge.calendar_at`.
- **Endless growth (§5.7)** — `sim/endless.py`, seeded, day-by-day, ramped; `bridge.endless_run`.
- **Offers (§5.8)** — `progression.offers(save, city, week)`, seeded on (owned, city, week);
  `bridge.offers_list`. Plus `tutorial_load`/`tutorial_run` (city editions via `sim/tutorial.py`).

## Debug log (the three that ate time)

1. `bridge.start/hand_start` didn't forward `pressure` — rings silently absent in stepped runs.
2. `step_result`'s `run(until=None)` + self-rescheduling TICK = event-budget explosion; fixed with
   `Scheduler.horizon` as the tick bound.
3. TICK reschedule bounded by a *step's* `until` killed the heartbeat mid-step → stepped ≠ full;
   ticks now reschedule to the next boundary bounded by the horizon only ([[Decision Log]]).
