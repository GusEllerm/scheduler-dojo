# 2026-10-02 Phase 2 Art 6 — weeks, offers as buildings, free grants

Art 6 lands in two parts. The **engine slice** (`6f50c02`: `BUILDINGS`, `offer_accept`,
`progression_grant`, save v3 `weeks`, city 4–9 scripts) was already shipped and noted in
[[Modules/scheduler_dojo-progression]]. **This note is part a — the UI half**: the week-end freeze,
the ONE offers overlay, building sprites with first-use guidance, and the tutorial's free grants.
Mapping authority stays in [[Concepts/Campus]] (see its "Week end and buildings on the campus"
section); beat grammar in [[Concepts/Tutorial]]; dialog/hatch channels in [[Concepts/Accessibility]].

## What shipped

- **`web/src/offers.ts`** — the single offers overlay both the campus and the tutorial open:
  each offer rendered as an inspectable *building* (name + blurb + "`reserve` tier of Kata"
  unlocks line, all from `progression_view.upgrades` — the same `BUILDINGS` table the sprites
  read). `onTake` verdicts land in a `role="status"` note; a successful take closes the panel.
- **`web/src/campus-play.ts`** — `weekTick` (engine `calendar_at` boundary + snapshot `week`),
  the traffic freeze in BOTH live and hand modes, `openWeekOffers`/`takeOffer`/`resolveWeek`,
  the pending-offers hatch, `refreshBuildings` → `LayoutInput.buildings`, `revealBuilding` as a
  real reveal (dim `?` → sprite + CSS pop), `tutorialManaged(on)`, first-use guidance gated by
  `buildingSeen:<id>` prefs, and `pendingFinish` so a run that drains at the boundary lands its
  result only after the choice. Everything is inert when `manual: true` (the visual harness),
  which is why `campus_visual` re-renders level1/level3 with **zero PNG diff** against the Art 3
  baselines.
- **`web/src/campus.ts` / `campus-render.ts` / `campus-hit.ts`** — `BuildingSprite` layout by
  engine anchor (locker by the lots, scale hut + truck pad on the road kerb, board by the
  neighbourhoods, gate at the edge), flat token sprites, building hit → detail card
  (`name — blurb + buildingHint`).
- **`web/src/tutorial.ts`** — `offer_upgrade {forced}` → the FREE `progression_grant` (the
  "not buyable" purchase message is gone); `{pick_of: 2}` → the campus's own
  `openWeekOffers` at the boundary week; gate/wait release on `week_end` (see
  [[Concepts/Tutorial]] — the hand clock stops at its last arrival, so an unreachable gate would
  strand the script); `tutorialManaged` hand-off on attach/finish.
- **`web/src/bridge.ts` / `progression.ts`** — `offerAccept`/`progressionGrant` wrappers
  (dispatch `offer_accept` / `progression_grant`), `grant`/`acceptOffer` store helpers, the
  `weeks` field on `ProgressionState`, `buildings` on `ProgressionView`.

## Decisions

- **The freeze fires the `week_end` event.** Hand snapshots pin `week: 1` (`hand_start`/`hand_tick`
  don't register the level for the snapshot calendar), and the arrival stream can end before the
  horizon, so the runner's old `now >= duration` clause alone would miss the boundary while the
  campus stood frozen. The freeze IS the engine boundary, so it announces it (event dedupe makes
  the two paths harmless together). Alternative rejected: a Python change to hand snapshots
  ("Python untouched" this slice).
- **Week end releases pending tutorial waits** — `[agent decision]`, rationale in
  [[Concepts/Tutorial]].
- **`pendingFinish`**: a live run that reaches the boundary and `done` in the same batch shows
  offers first, then the result — "never end a turn broken".

# Part b — campaign chaining, the fairness rail, the 820 px pass

Part b closes Art 6 on the client: the campaign now **chains city → city**, the §2.4 fairness
building gets its **share meters**, and the campus survives **820 px**. Mapping authority stays in
[[Concepts/Campus]] ("The fairness rail, the chain, and 820 px") and [[Concepts/Tutorial]]
("The chain"). Python untouched; goldens untouched.

## What shipped

- **`web/src/tutorial.ts`** — `TutorialScript.end` grew `endless_unlock`; `finish(status, completed)`
  reports the ending through `TutorialOptions.onEnd({city, next, endlessUnlock, completed})`, and
  ONLY from a real ending (`then: [{end: true}]` or the last step). A skipped script and a teardown
  `destroy()` end a script without finishing a city — neither may offer the chain.
- **`web/src/main.ts`** — the "Next city ▸" chip (hidden until a chain is pending) re-runs
  `startCampusRun()` with `campusCity`/`campusVariant = "hand"`/`campusTutorial = true`: the next
  city's *edition*, a fresh hand campus, its script attached, `prefs.city` remembering the frontier,
  the tutorial chip re-labelling itself. `endless_unlock` flips a separate **Endless ▸** availability
  chip (aria-disabled; Art 7 launches the mode) — also derived from the campaign city so the
  hand-off path into city 4+ shows it. A booth hand-off out of a HAND city (levels 1-2 have no kata
  mode) gets a "◀ Back to campus" chip so the editor cannot strand the chain; `modeAllowed("campus")`
  stopped being level-bound (hand cities are 1-2 only in the *watch* picker); and a campus run now
  records completion against `run.level_id` (a city edition scores against `level6`, not whatever
  the picker last loaded).
- **`web/src/campus.ts`** — `fairnessShares(jobs, now)` + `FairnessShare`: served
  (`min(end, now) - start`, clamped) vs claimed (`est`, the same length the vehicle paints) summed per
  sorted user id, integer seconds, shares of the totals, and a `starved` verdict at "served under
  half the share it submitted". Deterministic and pure — the engine's own `fairness` metric is
  untouched as the score's truth.
- **`web/src/campus-play.ts`** — `CampusPlayOptions.rail` (optional; the harness never gets one) and
  `paintFairness`: one card, `role="list"`, one `li` per neighbour with the OWNER token swatch +
  label, a served-share bar with the entitlement tick at the submitted share, and a text sentence
  carrying every number. `fairnessPinned` = owned-and-revealed, or a `reveal {building: fairness}`
  beat (which now also sets `fairnessFlashed`), else the rail stays `hidden` — and a hidden rail
  means the canvas keeps its full width.
- **`web/src/style.css`** — `.campus-body` (canvas column + rail), the `.campus-fairness` block
  (`warn` token marker = a ◆ glyph *and* the word "starved"), the two chips, and the ≤860 px block:
  rail under the canvas, control/hand/variant bars wrap, offers one card per row.

## Decisions and deviations

- **The rail is the shell's, not the campus's.** `CampusPlay` paints into `opts.rail`; the visual
  harness passes none, so no campus pixel moved — `node scripts/campus_visual.mjs --levels
  level1,level3` re-rendered **byte-identical** PNGs (verified with `cmp` against the pre-run copies).
  Alternatives rejected: an absolutely positioned overlay over the canvas (overlaps the map at 820)
  and putting the rail inside the stage (shrinks `canvas.parentElement` → every baseline moves).
- **The chain chip lives in the campus toolbar**, not in a modal: the ending already unfroze the
  campus, and a modal would compete with the offers panel. `[agent decision]`
- **`fairnessShares` is client-side** (the brief says compute in TS from the jobs list). The engine
  keeps the score's `fairness` metric; the meter is presentation of facts the snapshot already
  carries, and its "starved under half a share" verdict is presentation grammar, not a rule.
- Deviation from the deliverable's evidence wording: the campaign drive is committed as
  `web/scripts/art6b_evidence.mjs` rather than thrown away, so the log above is re-runnable; it is
  not wired into CI (it is a playtest rig, not a gate).
- `recordCompletion` against `run.level_id` was not asked for; chaining made the old behaviour wrong
  (six city runs all recorded as `level1`), so it is fixed here with a default argument that leaves
  watch/hand/kata untouched.
